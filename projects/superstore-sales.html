Superstore Sales Analytics

Interactive Tableau dashboard exploring sales by product category, region, and customer segment, with KPI summaries, trend analysis, forecasting, and shipping-efficiency views.

TableauDashboard DesignKPI AnalysisForecasting

The question

What patterns in sales performance can help explain differences across products, regions, and customer segments?

Analytical approach

  • Organized sales data for interactive exploration by category, region, and customer segment.
  • Built KPI summaries and trend-focused dashboard views.
  • Used forecasting and visual analysis to explore sales performance and shipping efficiency.

Findings & outcomes

  • Dashboard supports exploration of sales patterns across several business dimensions.
  • Demonstrates interactive business intelligence reporting and visual storytelling.

Limitations & interpretation

The dashboard is intended for exploratory analysis; patterns should be interpreted in the context of the underlying dataset and its time coverage.
