Freight Brokerage Load Analysis

CRISP-DM analysis of freight-load profitability using exploratory analysis, feature engineering, preprocessing, regression, tree-based models, and clustering.

PythonCRISP-DMFeature EngineeringRandom ForestK-Means

The question

Which factors help explain load margin, and how could predictive analysis support freight-pricing and operational decisions?

Analytical approach

  • Applied CRISP-DM to a logistics dataset, including exploratory analysis and preprocessing.
  • Compared Linear Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and K-Means approaches.
  • Examined revenue, cost structure, market conditions, and shipment geography.

Findings & outcomes

  • Random Forest was selected as the best-performing model, with R² = 0.65.
  • Carrier revenue, cost structure, market conditions, and shipment geography were identified as important profitability drivers.
  • Potential applications include pricing support, carrier selection, and load prioritization.

Limitations & interpretation

Operational deployment would require validation on new data, monitoring, and integration with brokerage workflows.
