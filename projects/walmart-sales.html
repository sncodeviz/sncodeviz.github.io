Walmart Sales Forecasting

CRISP-DM project examining weekly retail sales across more than 45 stores, combining exploratory analysis, hypothesis testing, and predictive modeling.

PythonpandasRandom ForestRegressionCRISP-DM

The question

Can historical store-level and economic indicators help explain and forecast weekly retail sales?

Analytical approach

  • Built a CRISP-DM workflow in Python and cleaned and engineered more than 6,400 records.
  • Conducted hypothesis testing, with a reported p-value of 0.0076.
  • Compared a baseline regression model with a Random Forest model.
  • Examined Store ID, CPI, and unemployment as important drivers.

Findings & outcomes

  • Random Forest achieved R² = 0.93 with RMSE of approximately $147K.
  • Baseline regression achieved R² = 0.15.
  • Random Forest performed substantially better in this project's evaluation.

Limitations & interpretation

Model performance is specific to the project data and evaluation setup. Historical test performance does not guarantee future accuracy.
