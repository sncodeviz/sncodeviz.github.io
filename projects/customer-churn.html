Customer Churn Analysis

Excel case study using an interactive dashboard to examine customer churn patterns and identify segments that may warrant closer retention attention.

ExcelPivotTablesSlicersDynamic ChartsKPI Analysis

The question

What customer patterns and segments may help a business understand churn risk and focus retention analysis?

Analytical approach

  • Cleaned and transformed customer data using Excel tools and formulas.
  • Built PivotTables, slicers, and dynamic charts for interactive exploration.
  • Used KPI tracking and segment analysis to examine churn patterns.

Findings & outcomes

  • Dashboard highlights early churn-risk segments for further investigation.
  • Demonstrates practical Excel-based analysis and interactive reporting.

Limitations & interpretation

The case study identifies patterns in the supplied data; it does not establish that any single factor causes customers to leave.
